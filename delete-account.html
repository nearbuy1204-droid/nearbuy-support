Nearbuy Account Deletion

Users can request account deletion directly within the Nearbuy app.

Steps to delete your account:

  1. Open the Nearbuy app
  2. Go to: Settings → Account → Delete Account
  3. Confirm deletion

When you delete your account, all related data including your profile, listings, and messages will be permanently removed from our system.
